# Episode concepts

These are research candidates. The hooks are written to be true based on general knowledge, but every claim must still be checked against sources before it goes into a script.

Difficulty is about animation effort with our current toolkit. **Low:** mostly existing components. **Medium:** one or two new components or a more detailed animal. **High:** complex procedural geometry such as weaving or dense tunnel networks.

**Runners-up:** sociable weaver communal nest, honeybee comb, leafcutter ant fungus gardens, weaverbird woven nest (high difficulty: weaving), and an emperor penguin "no nest at all" contrast episode.

## Recommended pilot: beaver lodge

- **Strongest story chain.** Predators lead to an underwater entrance, and breathing air leads to a dry chamber. A sealed room raises the need for a vent; winter brings the frozen mud shell; and frozen water leads to the food cache. The dam payoff explains why the front door stays underwater, and it separates the lodge from the dam.
- **Well documented.** The National Park Service, Hinterland Who's Who, state wildlife agencies, and university extension services describe the same core structure.
- **Easiest to animate.** Simple shapes (a mound, water, sticks, tunnels) built from components that already exist after the cutaway test.
- **Iconic.** Instantly recognizable, a strong first impression for the channel.
